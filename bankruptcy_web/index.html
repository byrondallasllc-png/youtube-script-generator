📋 Your Personalized Next Steps

Chapter 7 — Liquidation Bankruptcy

3–6 Months

📌 What Is Chapter 7?

Chapter 7 is called "liquidation bankruptcy." A court-appointed trustee can sell your non-exempt property to repay creditors — but in most cases there's nothing to sell, and your eligible debts get completely erased.

Key benefit Most unsecured debt (credit cards, medical bills, personal loans) is wiped out entirely — typically in just 3–6 months.

✅ Best For People Who...

  • Have mostly unsecured debt (credit cards, medical bills)
  • Have little to no non-exempt property
  • Pass the Means Test (lower income)
  • Want the fastest path to a fresh start

⛔ Not Ideal If You...

  • Are behind on mortgage & want to save your home
  • Have significant non-exempt assets to protect
  • Fail the Means Test (income too high)
  • Filed Chapter 7 in the last 8 years

📊 Key Facts

Filing Fee$338
Duration3–6 months
Income RequiredLow/none preferred
Credit Report10 years from filing
Refile Wait8 yrs after prior Ch.7
Legal Ref.11 U.S.C. §§ 701–784

🚫 What Debts Survive Chapter 7?

These debts are NOT erased by bankruptcy (per 11 U.S.C. § 523):

🎓 Student loans (rare exceptions)
👶 Child support & alimony
🏛️ Recent income taxes (<3 yrs)
⚖️ Debts from fraud or wrongdoing
🚨 Criminal fines & restitution
🚗 DUI injury/death judgments

Chapter 13 — Repayment Plan

3–5 Years

📌 What Is Chapter 13?

Chapter 13 is called "reorganization bankruptcy." You propose a 3–5 year repayment plan to pay back some or all of your debts. You keep all your property throughout the process.

Key benefit You can save your home from foreclosure by catching up on mortgage arrears over the plan term — something Chapter 7 cannot do.

✅ Best For People Who...

  • Are behind on mortgage & want to save their home
  • Have non-exempt assets they want to keep
  • Have income too high for Chapter 7
  • Need to pay back taxes or support arrears
  • Filed Chapter 7 in the last 8 years

📊 Key Facts

Filing Fee$313
Duration3–5 years
Income RequiredYes — must have regular income
Credit Report7 years from filing
Unsecured Debt Limit< $465,275
Secured Debt Limit< $1,395,875
Legal Ref.11 U.S.C. §§ 1301–1330

⛔ Not Available If You...

  • Have no steady income source
  • Exceed the secured/unsecured debt limits
  • Are a corporation or LLC (individuals only)
  • Have unfiled tax returns for the past 4 years

💡 How the Repayment Plan Works

Your plan pays debts in a specific order of priority:

1️⃣
Priority Debts
Paid 100%: child support, recent taxes, admin costs
2️⃣
Secured Debts
Mortgage arrears, car loans (possibly at reduced rate)
3️⃣
Unsecured Debts
Credit cards, medical bills — often pennies on the dollar, remainder discharged

FeatureChapter 7Chapter 13

Co-signer ProtectionNoneCo-debtor stay available
Disposable IncomeYou keep itMust pay to creditors

Choose Chapter 7 if...

  • Mostly credit cards, medical bills, personal loans
  • Few non-exempt assets
  • Low income or you pass the Means Test
  • You want this resolved in months, not years

Choose Chapter 13 if...

  • Behind on mortgage and want to keep the home
  • Assets you'd lose in Chapter 7
  • Income too high to qualify for Chapter 7
  • Significant tax or support debt to repay over time
